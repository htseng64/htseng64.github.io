Rhythm, Stress & Intonation in Health English
🎯 Use appropriate rhythm, stress, and intonation when presenting health data and nursing concepts.

Stress Key Health Information
強調關鍵健康資訊
Place vocal stress on numbers, statistics, and key terms to aid comprehension.
在數字、統計數據和關鍵術語上加重語調以幫助理解。
This affects OVER people in Taiwan — that is percent of the total population.

Use Pauses for Impact
使用暫停增強效果
Pause deliberately before and after key health facts to let them resonate.
在關鍵健康事實前後刻意暫停，讓其充分傳達。
The mortality rate from in Taiwan is… [pause] … times higher than the OECD average.
台灣___的死亡率是……【暫停】……比OECD平均高___倍。
cardiovascular diseasediabetes complicationspreventable hospital infectionsuicidal crisis without intervention
nearly twoalmost three1.5more than two
I want you to pause for a moment and think about… [pause] …how many patients you will see in your career who suffer from .
我希望您暫停一下，想想……【暫停】……在您的職業生涯中，您將看到多少患有___的病人。
preventable chronic diseaseundiagnosed mental illnessnutrition-related illnessa fall injury

Use Rising and Falling Intonation
使用上升和下降語調
Signal questions, lists, and completed thoughts through intonation patterns.
透過語調模式表示問句、列舉和完整想法。
Can we afford to ignore ? [rising intonation] No. [falling] We cannot. [falling, emphatic]
我們能忽視___嗎？【上升語調】不。【下降】我們不能。【下降，強調】

Control Speaking Pace
控制說話語速
Vary your pace deliberately — slow for data, faster for background.
刻意調整語速——數據時放慢，背景資訊時加快。
[Slow, clear pace] According… to… the… Ministry… of… Health… and… Welfare… the prevalence… is… percent.
【緩慢清晰的語速】根……據……衛……生……福……利……部……盛行率……為……___%。
8.412.925nearly 30
[Normal pace for context] This issue has been growing for over a decade, but [slow for impact] the NURSING response has NOT kept pace.
