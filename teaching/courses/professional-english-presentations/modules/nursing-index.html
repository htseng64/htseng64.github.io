Wk 1 · U1
Welcome and Introducing Yourself
歡迎與自我介紹
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能用英文流暢地問候聽眾、介紹自己的姓名、科系與報告主題 Greet the audience, introduce your name, department, and topic in fluent English
PatternExample
Good morning/afternoon, everyone. Good morning, everyone. Welcome to my presentation.
My name is ___, and I'm a nursing student at ___. My name is Amy Chen, and I'm a nursing student at Hungkuang University.
Today, I'd like to talk to you about ___. Today, I'd like to talk to you about diabetes prevention in elderly patients.
I hope my presentation will help you ___. I hope my presentation will help you understand the importance of early screening.
🏥 Nursing Example
"Good afternoon, everyone. My name is Lin Wei-Ting, a third-year nursing student. Today, I'd like to talk to you about the impact of sleep deprivation on patient recovery — a topic that directly affects our daily clinical practice."
大家午安。我叫林薇婷，護理系三年級學生。今天我想和各位談談睡眠不足對病人康復的影響——一個直接影響我們每日臨床實踐的主題。
🌍
Wk 2 · U2
Introducing the Subject
介紹簡報主題
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能清楚宣佈主題、提供背景資訊、連結 SDG 永續發展目標 Announce your topic, give background context, and link it to an SDG goal
PatternExample
The subject of my presentation is ___. The subject of my presentation is hand hygiene compliance in ICU settings.
This topic is important because ___. This topic is important because HAIs affect 1 in 10 hospitalised patients globally.
This connects to SDG Goal ___, which focuses on ___. This connects to SDG Goal 3, which focuses on Good Health and Well-Being.
🏥 Nursing Example
"The subject of my presentation is the role of nurses in preventing hospital-acquired infections. This is closely linked to SDG Goal 3 — Good Health and Well-Being — as infections remain a leading cause of preventable deaths worldwide."
我的簡報主題是護士在預防醫院感染中的角色。這與SDG第三目標「良好健康與福祉」密切相關，因為感染仍是全球可預防死亡的主要原因。

Wk 3 · U3
Attention Grabber
吸引注意力
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 用統計數字、病人故事或反問句在 30 秒內抓住聽眾注意力 Hook your audience in 30 seconds using a statistic, patient story, or rhetorical question
PatternExample
Did you know that ___? Did you know that over 422 million people worldwide have diabetes?
Imagine you are a patient who ___. Imagine you are a patient who wakes up after surgery in intense pain — would you know how to ask for help?
According to ___, every ___ seconds, ___. According to the WHO, every two minutes a mother dies during childbirth.
🏥 Nursing Example
"Did you know that in Taiwan, over 2.3 million people are living with diabetes? As future nurses, we will care for these patients every single day. So — how prepared are we?"
你知道嗎？在台灣，超過230萬人正與糖尿病共存。身為未來的護士，我們每天都將照護這些病患。那麼——我們準備好了嗎？

Wk 4 · U4
Introducing the Objective
介紹簡報目標
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能清楚陳述簡報目的、範圍，並說明與護理的關聯性 State the aim, define the scope, and explain the nursing relevance
PatternExample
The aim of this presentation is to ___.The aim of this presentation is to explore evidence-based wound care practices.
By the end of this talk, you will be able to ___.By the end of this talk, you will be able to identify three risk factors for pressure ulcers.
This is relevant to nursing because ___.This is relevant to nursing because we are the first line of patient assessment.
🏥 Nursing Example
"The aim of this presentation is to examine how nurses can improve patient medication adherence. By the end, you will be able to apply three communication strategies in your clinical placement."
本簡報的目的是探討護士如何提高病患的服藥依從性。結束後，您將能夠在臨床實習中應用三種溝通策略。
📋
Wk 5 · U5
Outlining the Structure
列出簡報大綱
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能用英文清楚說明三段式簡報架構（問題-證據-解決） Outline a three-part structure: Problem → Evidence → Solution
PatternExample
My presentation is divided into ___ parts.My presentation is divided into three parts.
First, I'll cover ___. Then, ___. Finally, ___.First, I'll cover the problem. Then, I'll present the evidence. Finally, I'll propose solutions.
Each section will take approximately ___ minutes.Each section will take approximately one and a half minutes.
🏥 Nursing Example
"My presentation is divided into three parts. First, I'll describe the problem of nurse burnout in Taiwan. Then, I'll present research evidence on its effects on patient safety. Finally, I'll suggest strategies hospitals can adopt to support nursing staff."
我的簡報分為三個部分。首先，我將描述台灣護士倦怠的問題。然後，我將呈現其對病患安全影響的研究證據。最後，我將提出醫院可以採取的策略。
🎵
Wk 6 · U6
Rhythm, Stress and Intonation
節奏、重音、語調
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能用重音、停頓與語調變化強調醫護關鍵資訊 Stress key health terms, use pauses for impact, and apply rising/falling intonation
PatternTip
Stress the KEY word in each phrase."The most CRITICAL factor is hand hygiene." (stress CRITICAL)
Use a pause (…) before important data."The infection rate was… forty percent."
Rising intonation for lists, falling for conclusions."We checked temperature↗, blood pressure↗, and oxygen levels↘."
🎙 Delivery Practice
"This is NOT just a statistic. (pause) This represents a REAL patient. (pause) A patient who trusted US with their life."
這不只是一個數字。（停頓）這代表一位真實的病患。（停頓）一位把生命託付給我們的病患。
📊
Wk 7 · U7
Introducing the Main Body
簡報主要內容
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能引用健康數據來源、描述趨勢、比較並說明醫護意義 Cite sources, describe a health trend, and contextualise data for nursing
PatternExample
According to ___, ___.According to the WHO 2023 report, cardiovascular disease remains the leading cause of death globally.
The data shows a ___ trend in ___.The data shows an upward trend in Type 2 diabetes among people under 40.
This means that nurses need to ___.This means that nurses need to prioritise metabolic screening in younger patients.
🏥 Nursing Example
"According to Taiwan's Ministry of Health and Welfare, hypertension affects 26% of adults over age 18. The data shows a steady increase over the past decade. This means nurses must integrate blood pressure screening into every routine assessment."
根據台灣衛生福利部的資料，高血壓影響了26%的18歲以上成年人。數據顯示過去十年持續上升。這意味著護士必須將血壓篩查納入每次常規評估。
👁️
Wk 8 · U8
Eye Contact & Body Language
簡報肢體語言
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能維持眼神接觸、運用有目的性的手勢、控制台上姿勢與走位 Maintain eye contact, use purposeful gestures, and control posture and movement
StrategyHow to Do It
3-second eye contact ruleHold eye contact with one person for 3 seconds, then move to another. Don't read from slides.
Open palm gesturesUse open palms to signal inclusion: "As you can see on this slide…" (gesture toward screen)
Confident standing postureFeet shoulder-width apart, slight forward lean, avoid swaying or crossing arms.
💡 Self-Assessment Tip
Ask AI: "I will deliver this sentence: 'Nurse burnout affects one in three nurses.' What eye contact pattern and gesture should I use for maximum impact?"
問 AI：「我將說這句話：護士倦怠影響三分之一的護士。我應該使用什麼眼神接觸和手勢以達到最大效果？」

Wk 9 · U9
Elaborating and Giving Examples
簡報解釋與舉例
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能介紹臨床案例、發展案例細節、使用健康類比來解釋 Introduce a clinical example, develop its details, and use a health analogy
PatternExample
Let me give you an example. ___.Let me give you an example. Consider a 72-year-old patient admitted for a hip fracture.
To put this another way, ___.To put this another way, think of a blood vessel like a garden hose — blockages reduce flow.
In other words, ___.In other words, early detection can reduce mortality by up to 40%.
🏥 Nursing Example
"Let me give you a clinical example. Consider Mrs. Wang, a 68-year-old diabetic patient admitted for foot ulcer care. Initially, her wound showed no signs of healing. However, after our nursing team adjusted her dressing protocol and blood sugar monitoring, she was discharged within two weeks."
讓我舉一個臨床例子。想像王太太，一位68歲的糖尿病患者，因足部潰瘍入院治療。起初，她的傷口沒有癒合跡象。但在我們護理團隊調整換藥方案和血糖監測後，她在兩週內出院。
🧭
Wk 10 · U10
Signpost Language
簡報引導語言
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能用路標語言開啟新段落、強調重點、前後段連結 Open new sections, emphasise key points, and link back/forward with signpost language
FunctionPhrases
Moving on…"Now let's move on to the second part of my presentation."
Emphasising"I'd like to stress that hand hygiene is non-negotiable."
Linking back"As I mentioned earlier, the infection rate doubled in three years."
Linking forward"This leads us to my final point about nursing interventions."
🏥 Nursing Example
"Now let's move on to the evidence. As I mentioned, the problem is significant. I'd like to stress that the following data comes from peer-reviewed clinical research. This leads us directly to the solutions I'll propose."
現在讓我們進入證據部分。正如我提到的，問題是重大的。我要強調，以下數據來自同行評審的臨床研究。這直接引導我們到我將提出的解決方案。
📈
Wk 10 · U11
Using Visual Aids
使用輔助圖表
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能介紹圖表、描述趨勢、解釋護理意涵 Introduce a visual, describe a trend, and interpret it for nursing implications
PatternExample
If you look at this graph/chart, you can see ___.If you look at this bar chart, you can see that obesity rates have tripled since 1990.
The ___ line represents ___.The blue line represents the control group, while the red line shows the intervention group.
What this means for nursing is ___.What this means for nursing is that we need to implement earlier lifestyle counselling.
🏥 Nursing Example
"If you look at this pie chart, you can see that 45% of hospital falls occur during night shifts. The dark blue section represents falls with injury. What this means for nursing is that we must improve nighttime safety protocols and call-light response times."
如果您看這個圓餅圖，可以看到45%的住院跌倒發生在夜班。深藍色部分代表有受傷的跌倒。這對護理的意義是，我們必須改善夜間安全協議和呼叫燈反應時間。

Wk 11 · U12
Conclusion
簡報結論
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能總結要點、重申 SDG 連結、發出護理行動倡議 Summarise key points, restate the SDG link, and issue a call to nursing action
PatternExample
In conclusion, I have shown that ___.In conclusion, I have shown that nurse-led education significantly improves diabetes control.
The key takeaway is ___.The key takeaway is that every nurse can make a measurable difference through patient education.
As future nurses, I urge you to ___.As future nurses, I urge you to champion evidence-based wound care in your clinical practice.
🏥 Nursing Example
"In conclusion, I have shown that falls prevention is a shared nursing responsibility. The key takeaway is that a simple three-step assessment can reduce fall rates by 60%. As future nurses, I urge you to make patient safety your personal commitment — starting on day one of your clinical placement."
總而言之，我已經說明跌倒預防是護理的共同責任。關鍵收穫是，一個簡單的三步驟評估可以將跌倒率降低60%。身為未來的護士，我敦促您將病患安全作為個人承諾——從臨床實習的第一天開始。
❓
Wk 12 · U13
Inviting Questions & Responding
問題與回應
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能專業地邀請提問、複述問題、用證據回答 Invite questions professionally, acknowledge and repeat the question, and answer with evidence
PatternExample
I'd now like to open the floor for questions.I'd now like to open the floor for questions. Please feel free to ask anything.
That's a great question. If I understand correctly, you're asking ___.That's a great question. If I understand correctly, you're asking about the long-term outcomes.
I don't have the data on that, but I can research it and follow up.I don't have the specific figure on that, but I can research it and follow up with you by email.
🏥 Nursing Example
"I'd now like to open the floor for questions. (Audience asks: 'How do nurses deal with patients who refuse medication?') That's a great question. If I understand correctly, you're asking about patient refusal. Based on our ethics training, we must first explore the reasons for refusal, then involve the care team and document everything."
我現在想開放提問。（聽眾問：護士如何處理拒絕服藥的病患？）這是個很好的問題。如我理解，您是在問拒絕用藥的情況。根據我們的倫理培訓，我們必須先了解拒絕的原因，然後讓護理團隊介入並記錄一切。
⏱️
Wk 12 · U14
Time Management
時間控制
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能規劃 5 分鐘時間分配，並優雅地處理超時或未達時情況 Plan your 5-min allocation and gracefully manage overtime and undertime
SituationLanguage
Standard 5-min planIntro (30s) → Background (45s) → Evidence (90s) → Solution (45s) → Conclusion (30s) → Q&A (30s)
If running over time"I'm aware of the time, so let me skip to my key conclusion."
If finishing early"I have a few moments remaining — I'd like to share one final clinical insight."
⏱ 5-Minute Blueprint
0:00–0:30 → Greeting + Hook | 0:30–1:15 → Topic + Background | 1:15–2:45 → Evidence + Data | 2:45–3:30 → Nursing Solution | 3:30–4:00 → Conclusion + Call-to-Action | 4:00–5:00 → Q&A
0:00–0:30 問候+抓住注意力 | 0:30–1:15 主題+背景 | 1:15–2:45 證據+數據 | 2:45–3:30 護理解決方案 | 3:30–4:00 結論+行動倡議 | 4:00–5:00 問答

Wk 13 · U15
Thank You & Goodbye
致謝與道別
🎯 週目標：Weekly Goal: 能真誠致謝、重申核心訊息、以最終行動倡議完美收尾 Thank sincerely, restate your core message, and end with a final call to action
PatternExample
Thank you so much for your time and attention.Thank you so much for your time and attention today.
I hope this presentation has shown you that ___.I hope this presentation has shown you that nurses are the backbone of patient recovery.
Remember: ___. Thank you.Remember: every small action in nursing has the power to save a life. Thank you.
🏆 Perfect Closing Formula
"Thank you so much for your attention today. I hope this presentation has shown you that mental health support is as vital as physical care in nursing. Remember: when we care for the whole person, healing truly begins. Thank you, and I wish you all success in your nursing journey."
非常感謝您今天的關注。我希望這個簡報向您說明，心理健康支持在護理中與身體護理同樣重要。記住：當我們關心整個人，真正的癒合才開始。謝謝您，祝您在護理旅途上一切順利。
