Essential Skills

1

Environmental Assessment

Notice which parts of a care environment — light, noise, color, greenery — are affecting a specific patient's comfort and recovery.

2

3

Healing Environment · One Element

Color Therapy

Chromotherapy is the use of color and light to support mood, comfort, and physiological calm. In nursing practice, it shows up mostly through deliberate choices in wall color, lighting tone, and decor — one small, controllable part of a healing environment.

Why It Matters at the Bedside

Color influences perceived room temperature, alertness, and calm well before a patient consciously notices it. Hospitals increasingly weigh color psychology alongside light and layout — though the evidence works best combined with the other healing-environment factors, not as a stand-alone fix.

    How You'll Be Evaluated

    Environmental Nursing Intervention Rubric

    This is the same checklist used during the in-class design task and gallery walk. Each criterion is scored from Needs Improvement to Excellent.

    Criterion 1Needs Improvement 2Developing 3Good 4Excellent
    Clarity Intervention is not named or is unclear. Intervention is named but the reason is missing or vague. Intervention and reason are both stated clearly. Intervention and reason are stated clearly and precisely fitted to the patient.
    Evidence Alignment No connection to what was taught about healing environments. A loose or partial connection to course content. A clear, accurate connection to course content. A clear connection, plus reasoning about why it works for this specific case.
    Feasibility Not realistic for the patient's condition or setting. Somewhat realistic, with notable gaps. Realistic for the patient's condition and the ward setting. Realistic, and explicitly accounts for cost, safety, or timing constraints.
    Communication Difficult to follow; sentence frame not used. Understandable, but hesitant or unclear in places. Clear spoken English using the sentence frame. Clear, confident, and persuasive — could convince a colleague to act.
