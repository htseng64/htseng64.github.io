Core Course Framework

This course uses one simple logic chain. Nurses assess the care environment, predict the patient behavior it may trigger, and design safe interventions that support recovery.

Nature, water, light, color, quiet, air quality, layout, and social space.

Nursing perspective: we do not only provide treatment; we also design conditions that make healthy behavior easier.

Therapeutic Spaces

Click each tab. These concepts support your EMI demonstrations and the full 18-week course.

Green Space

Gardens, trees, indoor plants, and nature views can reduce stress, support mood, encourage walking, and create social connection.

Example intervention: supervised garden walk after lunch.

Blue Space

Water views, fountains, rivers, ponds, and coastal environments can support calmness, restoration, and emotional regulation.

Example intervention: safe seated rest near a water feature or nature video with water sounds.

White / Quiet Space

Quiet rooms, uncluttered design, and restorative pause areas reduce sensory overload and support attention, sleep, and reflection.

Example intervention: protected quiet hour with reduced alarms and dimmed light.

Color & Light

Soft blue, sage green, warm beige, daylight, and circadian-friendly lighting can influence perceived comfort and sleep-wake rhythm.

Example intervention: morning daylight exposure and calming color accents.
